About the Foundation

Connecting vision with action

A nonprofit created by Rochester Sports leadership to address current and future sports and recreation facility needs.

Building bridges for a better community

The Rochester Sports Foundation brings community, government, and private-sector partners together to make sports facility projects solid, sustainable, and responsive to local needs.

Our purpose

We lead planning and development efforts, provide a credible forum for community input, support facility improvements through grants, and help Rochester remain competitive as a sports destination.

Leadership

The complete migration will include current staff, Board of Directors, advisory participants, biographies, and approved contact information.

Established mission

Facilities. Partnerships. Opportunity.

A stronger facility network supports Rochester families, local athletes, economic development, and visitors from across the region.
