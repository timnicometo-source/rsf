Baseball Continues Its Home Run Tradition

Participation continues to rise as destinations across the country invest in tournament-ready diamonds, visitor amenities, and community partnerships.

Peter Francesconi • 10-minute read

Share

Facebook LinkedIn

Team sports participation in the United States rose sharply in the five years following the pandemic, climbing from 22.1 percent to 27.8 percent of the population and moving ahead of levels recorded a decade earlier.

Baseball and softball illustrate that momentum. Physical Activity Council research placed U.S. baseball participation at 17.3 million players age six and older in 2024, a 3.8 percent increase from the previous year and a 9.4 percent increase over five years.

Baseball participation remains strong

Communities across the country have responded with facilities and visitor services designed for youth, amateur, collegiate, and professional play.

Aurora, Illinois

The Aurora region's primary youth and amateur baseball and softball venue is Stuart Sports Complex in Montgomery. Its eight diamonds are arranged in two quads, with seven lighted fields, adaptable base paths, spectator seating, permanent restrooms, concessions, and parking for 1,300 vehicles. Families also have access to a dog park, playground, splash pad, and walking trails.

Nearby Northwestern Medicine Field in Geneva, home of the Kane County Cougars, provides a professional setting available for rentals. The Aurora Area Convention and Visitors Bureau supports organizers with attraction discounts, marketing assistance, promotions, and volunteer connections.

Columbia, South Carolina

The Columbia region offers an extensive collection of diamonds, led by the nine-field Lexington Sports Complex. Pine Grove, Oak Grove, Howard Park, Midland, and Gilbert add numerous lighted fields, while Lexington Baseball Stadium accommodates showcase games with seating for 3,000 spectators.

USSSA and Perfect Game events have each attracted approximately 200 teams. Experience Columbia SC Sports supports organizers with site visits, venue and hotel coordination, promotion, marketing, rights fees, and other event services.

Lake Charles, Louisiana

Lake Charles features 25 youth fields with turf infields, eight turf high-school fields, and an all-turf collegiate field. McMurry Park in Sulphur hosts the Louisiana high-school baseball championships and includes nine lighted youth fields, batting cages, concessions, and tournament amenities.

The Perfect Game 12U World Series brings more than 100 teams to the area annually. Visit Lake Charles assists organizers with facilities, lodging, and potential grant opportunities while promoting attractions for athletes and families.

Laredo, Texas

Laredo's baseball inventory includes 10 new diamonds at Buena Vista Sports Complex, additional fields at the United Independent School District Student Activity Complex and Laredo College, the 500-seat Haynes Baseball Field, and the 6,000-seat Uni-Trade Stadium.

The community has hosted Border Olympics, Pony Super Regional, and World Series events featuring domestic and international teams. Laredo's tourism and baseball organizations coordinate facilities, officials, coaches, tournament directors, and families to support event success.

Lincoln, Nebraska

Lincoln offers eight lighted diamonds at Sandhills Global Event Center, six fields at Optimist Youth Sports Complex, four baseball and softball fields at Densmore Park, and seven additional diamonds at Doris Bair Softball Complex. Haymarket Park provides an 8,500-seat showcase venue.

The facilities host NAIA baseball, state championships, Triple Crown tournaments, and youth events. Visit Lincoln supports organizers with venue sourcing, lodging, registration, welcome services, and potential financial assistance.

Memphis, Tennessee

Memphis and the surrounding Mid-South pair major baseball venues with strong event partnerships. USA Baseball Stadium includes nine fields and a 5,000-seat stadium; Snowden Grove Park has 17 lighted diamonds; and AutoZone Park provides a 10,000-seat professional setting.

Youth and high-school tournaments regularly attract more than 150 teams, complemented by summer collegiate exposure events. Memphis Tourism assists with hotels, volunteers, marketing, site visits, and visitor experiences.

Mobile, Alabama

Mobile's baseball heritage includes five Major League Baseball Hall of Famers and nearly a century of youth participation. West Mobile County Park offers eight youth diamonds, while the newer Land Sports and Recreation Complex in Saraland adds eight fields with turf outfields and an indoor sports building.

Westside Park and Semmes Community Park provide additional youth fields and visitor amenities. The Mobile Sports Authority partners with tournament organizers on logistics for state, regional, and national competition.

Myrtle Beach, South Carolina

Myrtle Beach welcomes youth teams from across the country for the Youth Baseball Nationals. Grand Park's Styers Athletic Complex, Ned Donkle Field Complex, The Ripken Experience, North Myrtle Beach Park and Sports Complex, Eight Oaks Park, and Coastal Carolina University's Spring Brooks Stadium provide options for nearly every competition level.

Long-running partnerships with Triple Crown Sports and The Ripken Experience have helped baseball remain the region's leading youth sport by participation while positioning the destination for continued growth.

A continuing American tradition

From flexible youth complexes to professional stadiums, communities are investing in the facilities and hospitality networks that allow baseball participation and tournament travel to grow.
