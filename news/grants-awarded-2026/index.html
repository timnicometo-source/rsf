$10,000 in Grants Awarded at Rochester Sports Banquet to Enhance Local Sports Facilities

Local Associations Receive Over $10,000 in Grants to Improve Rochester Sports Facilities

Rochester Sports Foundation • 3-minute read

Share

Facebook LinkedIn

The 20th Anniversary Rochester Sports Banquet, held Sunday, February 1, was a landmark occasion celebrating local sports excellence and community impact.

An annual tradition since 2005, the event brought together players, coaches, families, and community leaders to honor the top performances of the 2025 season. Beyond the individual accolades, the Foundation continued its core mission: supporting and advancing the infrastructure of local sports facilities.

“The banquet is our moment to pause and appreciate the immense talent and dedication within our local sports community.”

Chris Terry President, Rochester Sports Foundation

Terry added that the Foundation is proud the annual celebration also helps improve the facilities where Rochester athletes play.

$10,000+invested in local sports facilities
20 yearsof celebrating Rochester sports

Community investment with lasting value

Grant funding supports practical improvements that help local associations provide safer, stronger, and more welcoming places for athletes and spectators.

Supporting Rochester softball

Featured grant recipients included Brad and Clover Tiedeken of the Rochester Softball Association and Jen Stewart and Holly Schmitz of the Rochester Youth Fastpitch Softball Association, along with Foundation President Chris Terry.

Grant recipient

Rochester Softball Association

Brad and Clover Tiedeken represented the Rochester Softball Association as it received support for a needed scoreboard replacement at the McQuillan Softball Complex.

The complex welcomes hundreds of teams and visitors, from youth through adult competition, during each calendar year.

Projects supported by the grants

The two projects address practical facility needs while helping local softball organizations continue serving athletes throughout the Rochester area.

The Rochester Sports Foundation works with community partners to advance sports and recreation facilities throughout the region.
