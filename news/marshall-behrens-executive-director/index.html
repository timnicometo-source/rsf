Rochester Sports Foundation Names Marshall Behrens as New Executive Director

Behrens brings decades of leadership, community involvement, sports officiating, and facility-development experience to the Foundation.

Chris Terry • 4-minute read

Share

Facebook LinkedIn

The Rochester Sports Foundation announced the appointment of Marshall Behrens as executive director, succeeding longtime leader Ed Hruska following his retirement at the end of 2025.

Behrens brings a combination of leadership, community involvement, and deep sports experience to the role. A fixture in Rochester athletics, he has served as a sports official for more than 25 years in football, basketball, baseball, and softball at the high-school and collegiate levels.

He has also served on the Rochester Sports Board since 2015, including terms as treasurer and 2024 board president, helping guide the city's sports tourism strategy and event-development efforts.

“My goal is to build on the strong foundation Ed has created through the transformative work he has done and expand the Foundation's ability to generate support for Rochester's sports facilities and events in new and innovative ways.”

Marshall Behrens Executive Director, Rochester Sports Foundation

Leadership shaped by service

Alongside his volunteer service, Behrens built a professional career at Mayo Clinic and served as a research administrator for surgical specialties in Rochester. He credits both his career and officiating experience with strengthening his ability to lead teams, build consensus, and manage complex challenges under pressure.

“Marshall brings an authentic love for sports and a deep understanding of how strategic facility development fuels community growth.”

Chris Terry Board President, Rochester Sports Foundation

Terry described Behrens as a proven leader who understands how to unite people around projects that improve Rochester's sports infrastructure and economic vitality. Retiring Executive Director Ed Hruska added that Behrens already understood how strong facilities can strengthen community connections and generate long-term impact.

Building partnerships for future growth

Behrens identified stronger partnerships and new facility-funding approaches among his top priorities. He said the Foundation could play a pivotal role in maximizing Rochester's potential while building on what the community had already accomplished.

About the Rochester Sports Foundation

The Rochester Sports Foundation is a 501(c)(3) created to address current and future sports and recreation facility needs in the Rochester area. It builds connections among the community, government, and private sector to make facility projects strong and sustainable.
