Sports Tourism April 23, 2025

Something BIG Is Coming to Rochester

Rochester was selected as a host community for World Juniors pre-tournament hockey events in December 2025.

Rochester Sports Foundation • 3-minute read

Share

Facebook LinkedIn

On April 21, 2025, a press conference at Mayo Civic Center announced that the World Juniors Pre-Tournament Series Hockey Tournament was coming to Rochester in December.

Rochester was expected to host eight to 10 teams that would later compete in the World Juniors tournament in the Twin Cities. The city joined Bemidji and Mankato as Minnesota host communities for exhibition games leading up to the 2026 International Ice Hockey Federation World Junior Championship, held December 26, 2025 through January 5, 2026.

8-10teams expected in Rochester
10nations competing for gold
800+local youth hockey participants

A world-class hockey opportunity

The championship showcases the world's leading men's hockey players under age 20.

“This exciting opportunity brings future NHL stars to our own backyard, giving our community a front-row seat to world-class competition.”

Matt Esau Executive Director, Rochester Sports

Esau said the event would also allow Rochester to showcase itself to USA Hockey as a premier host city for future events, while recognizing the community members whose support helped make the opportunity possible.

A proud Rochester hockey tradition

Rochester's hockey tradition spans youth programs, national championship Junior teams, and athletes who have competed in college, the Olympics, and the professional ranks. Selection as a World Juniors host community represented another important chapter in that history.
