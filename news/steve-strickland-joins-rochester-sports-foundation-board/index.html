Steve Strickland Joins Rochester Sports Foundation Board

The longtime coach, activities administrator, and sports advocate brings 38 years of leadership experience to the Foundation's Board of Directors.

Rochester Sports Foundation • 3-minute read

Share

Facebook LinkedIn

The Rochester Sports Foundation appointed Steve Strickland to its Board of Directors, adding a familiar local athletics leader with decades of coaching, administrative, sales, and advocacy experience.

Strickland's appointment reflects a career-long dedication to improving the quality of youth and prep sports in the Rochester region. At the time of his appointment, he worked as a sales representative for Game One after previously serving as Activities Director for Rochester Catholic Schools.

His professional background also includes a long tenure in the paper industry, giving him experience across corporate management, educational administration, and athletics leadership.

Deep roots in Rochester athletics

Strickland and his family moved to Rochester in 1997. He went on to coach track and field at Rochester Lourdes High School for 15 years, while also coaching youth baseball, basketball, and football. Those experiences gave him a broad perspective on the needs of athletes at every developmental level.

Experience across sports and administration

Strickland's background spans coaching, education, corporate leadership, sales, and community advocacy.

A University of Wisconsin-Stevens Point graduate with a bachelor's degree in history, Strickland joined the Board with a particular interest in advocating for better local sports facilities and ensuring that future Rochester athletes have the resources needed to succeed.

Committed to Rochester's sporting community

The Rochester Sports Foundation welcomed Strickland's experience, community perspective, and commitment to the athletes and families who use local sports facilities.
